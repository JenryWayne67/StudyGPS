How it works

Three steps to a clearer semester

Step 01

Map your courses

Add your subjects, assessment weights, and due dates. StudyGPS builds a structured map of everything the semester expects from you.

Setup takes about 5 minutes

Step 02

Follow your route

Each day you get a short, prioritised list of study units — ordered by deadline pressure, subject weight, and how well you have retained the material.

Recalculated every morning

Step 03

Track real progress

Completion, coverage, and grade trends stay visible in one place, so you can see which subjects are on course and which need a detour.

Updated after every session
